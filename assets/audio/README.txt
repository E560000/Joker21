Optional JOKER 21 sound files

Put your audio files in this folder. OGG, WAV, and MP3 are supported; use one
file per sound with these names:

  card_draw.ogg       Card is dealt or drawn
  loss.ogg            Blackjack hand is lost or busted
  background_music.ogg  Looping background music
  win.ogg             Blackjack hand is won
  points.ogg          Chips or multiplier are added during scoring
  blind_select.ogg    Player selects Play Blind
  joker_buy.ogg       Joker is successfully bought in the shop
  joker_sell.ogg      Joker is successfully sold in the shop
  score_end.ogg       Scoring finishes at the end of a blackjack hand
  game_loss.ogg       Run ends in defeat
  click.ogg           UI control is clicked or activated
  new_game.ogg        A new run starts

The game also checks for matching .wav and .mp3 names. Files are optional; a
missing or unsupported sound is skipped without interrupting the game. The
points sound rises in pitch as scoring chains continue.
